# Gustaf Alströmer: Why Retention is the Only Metric That Matters (Lenny's Podcast)
**Guest:** Gustaf Alströmer
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Topic:** Retention Curves, Product-Market Fit Benchmarks, and Growth Loops vs. Funnels

---

## The Leaky Bucket: Why Growth Without Retention is Fake

[Timestamp: 03:50]
Lenny: "Gustaf, you ran growth at Airbnb and now you are a Group Partner at Y Combinator evaluating thousands of startups. What is the fundamental law of growth?"

Gustaf Alströmer: "The fundamental law is this: **Retention is the only metric that matters.** If you do not have retention, you do not have a company.

Most founders come to YC obsessing over acquisition. They spend $50,000 on Facebook ads, show a hockey-stick sign-up graph, and think they have product-market fit. But when you look at their cohort retention curves, the line heads straight to zero. That is a leaky bucket. Pouring more water into a bucket with a giant hole at the bottom just wastes cash."

---

## Reading Cohort Retention Curves

[Timestamp: 14:10]
Gustaf Alströmer: "To know if you have product-market fit, plot your cohorts: Month 1, Month 2, Month 3, Month 6, Month 12.
- If the curve continues downward toward the x-axis: You do not have PMF. Stop marketing. Fix the core product value proposition.
- If the curve flattens out and becomes horizontal parallel to the x-axis—even at 20% or 30%: You have a viable cohort of users who find recurring value. Now you have PMF for that sub-segment.

### Retention Benchmarks:
- **B2B SaaS:** Good is 40%–50% logo retention at 12 months; Great is Net Dollar Retention (NDR) $> 120\%$.
- **Consumer Social:** Good Day 30 retention is $> 20\%$; Great is $> 35\%$.
- **Marketplaces:** Look for repeat purchase frequency within 90 days."

## Growth Loops vs. Traditional Funnels

[Timestamp: 25:00]
Gustaf Alströmer: "Funnels are linear: Acquire -> Activate -> Convert -> Churn. In a funnel, every single user requires new energy and marketing spend.

Loops are compounding: User takes action -> Creates content or invites others -> Drives new users -> System compounds.
At Airbnb, guest virality was our greatest loop: a traveler rents an apartment, falls in love with the concept, returns home, and becomes a host. That single closed-loop mechanic fueled organic global growth without paid ad spend."

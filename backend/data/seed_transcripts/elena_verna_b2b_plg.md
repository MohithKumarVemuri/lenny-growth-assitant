## What Most Companies Get Wrong About PLG

[Timestamp: 05:40]
Lenny: "Elena, you’ve led and advised growth at Miro, Amplitude, SurveyMonkey, and Netlify. When companies say 'We want to be PLG', what is the biggest mistake you see?"

Elena Verna: "The number one mistake is thinking that PLG is just self-serve checkout. Founders build a pricing page with a $15/month credit card form, put their sales team on ice, and then wonder why enterprise revenue drops off a cliff.

PLG is not a pricing model; it is a distribution and go-to-market philosophy. It means the product itself is responsible for acquiring, activating, retaining, and expanding accounts. 

In enterprise B2B, PLG is the top-of-funnel engine that feeds **Product-Led Sales (PLS)**. You let end-users adopt the product for free to solve an immediate tactical headache. Once you reach critical density—say, 10 or 15 active users in a single corporate domain—your sales reps reach out not to pitch shelfware, but to sell security, compliance, and enterprise admin control to the CIO."

## Freemium vs. Free Trial: How to Decide

[Timestamp: 16:15]
Elena Verna: "Here is the exact decision framework I use:
1. **Choose Freemium when:**
   - Your product has strong inherent viral loops (e.g. Miro, Figma, Slack). When user A uses the product, they naturally invite user B to collaborate.
   - Your time-to-value is fast (under 5 minutes).
   - Your marginal cost of serving a free user is low.
   - Freemium keeps users engaged forever until an organizational trigger (like reaching team member caps or advanced security features) forces monetization.

2. **Choose Free Trial when:**
   - Your product is single-player or requires heavy configuration (e.g., complex data pipelines).
   - You need urgency to force an evaluation within 14 or 30 days.
   - Your cost-of-goods-sold (COGS) per user is high (e.g. heavy AI compute or data ingestion)."

## Product-Led Sales (PLS) and Product Qualified Leads (PQLs)

[Timestamp: 27:30]
Elena Verna: "Never let sales reps cold-call people who haven't experienced the 'Aha!' moment. That destroys your brand. 

Instead, define your **PQL (Product Qualified Lead)**:
- Demographic qualification: Company size > 100 employees, target industry.
- Behavioral qualification: Account has reached at least 3 collaborative projects, or invited 5 teammates within 14 days.
- Intent trigger: A free user hits a paywall or attempts to configure SSO/SAML.

When sales reps engage a PQL, the close rate is typically 3x to 5x higher than traditional outbound SDR leads because the customer is already in love with the software."
